Pricing FAQ

A few common questions that come up before we start.

What’s the difference between implementation and the monthly fee?

Implementation is the one-time project to design, build, and launch your bot: mapping flows, wiring tools, testing, and training. The monthly fee covers hosting, monitoring, and improvements — so your bot doesn’t slowly break as your business changes.

Can we start on Starter and move up later?

Yes. Many clients start on Starter for a single workflow, then move to Pro once they see results and want to add more channels, locations, or bots.

Do you offer discounts for agencies or multiple locations?

Yes. If you’re rolling out bots across multiple brands or locations, we can create a Scale package that makes sense for your volume and support needs.

What if I don’t know which bot or plan I need?

That’s exactly what the 15-minute intro call is for. We’ll talk through your current lead flow, tools, and goals — then recommend a simple starting point or tell you honestly if it’s not the right time.

Is there a long-term contract?

Most clients start month-to-month after implementation. If you prefer a longer commitment for better budgeting, we can discuss that too.
