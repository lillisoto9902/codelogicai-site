Pricing

CodeLogicAI pricing is designed around how you actually use AI: one clear bot or workflow at a time, with managed hosting and support layered on top.

The numbers below are a structure template — you can adjust exact amounts, discounts, and payment plans as you finalize your offers.

Core bot packages

🌱
Starter Bot Package
Ideal for a single bot focused on one clear job — like lead capture or basic intake.
  • 1 AI bot (CRM bot or CareClerk-lite, or similar).
  • Custom conversation flow design.
  • Connection to 1–2 tools (calendar, email, or sheet/CRM).
  • Launch support and basic training call.
  • Email support after launch.

Structure: one-time setup fee + monthly hosting/support.

Best for getting started
⚡
Pro Bot Stack
For teams ready to automate multiple parts of their workflow and keep everything in sync across tools.
  • Up to 2–3 coordinated bots (e.g., CRM bot + scheduling + CFO insights).
  • Deeper integrations (CRM, phone/SMS, web chat, shared inbox, etc.).
  • Custom routing, conditions, and business rules.
  • Team training sessions and written playbooks.
  • Priority support and periodic optimization.

Structure: higher setup fee + tiered monthly for hosting, monitoring, and ongoing improvements.

For growing teams
🏥
CareClerk & Healthcare Workflows
Designed for clinics, hospitals, and healthcare teams where intake, communication, and guardrails really matter.
  • CareClerk deployment (intake, reminders, FAQs, routing).
  • Multi-location or multi-provider logic if needed.
  • Policy-aware flows and escalation paths.
  • Staff training and usage SOPs.
  • Closer collaboration during rollout and refinement.

Structure: scoped proposal based on sites, volume, and integrations.
