About CodeLogicAI

We build AI bots that respect your time, your team, and your data.

CodeLogicAI is a boutique AI studio focused on one thing: turning messy, real-world workflows into practical automations — for owners, operators, and teams that don’t want another dashboard they never log into.

Built for small and growing teams

We work with home services, healthcare practices, local businesses, and lean agencies that need leverage, not more logins. Our bots capture leads, book appointments, update CRMs, and surface CFO-level insights in plain language.

Behind every automation is a real partnership: we co-design your flows, test them on your traffic, and iterate until they feel like an extension of your best team member — not a random chatbot bolted onto your site.

Our approach

We’re not trying to replace humans — we’re here to give them better tools. Every project is scoped around outcomes you care about, not vanity metrics.

Outcome-first

We start with your target: more booked jobs, fewer no-shows, cleaner books, or faster intake. The tech serves the outcome — never the other way around.

Simple to run

Your team shouldn’t need a prompt engineer. We document flows, create playbooks, and train your staff to own the system day-to-day.

Transparent automation

We show you exactly what’s automated, where data goes, and how to override or pause any flow. No black boxes — just clear levers.

How CodeLogicAI came together

CodeLogicAI grew out of hands-on work in healthcare, small business ops, and real-world service companies — not from a lab or a pitch deck.

Step 1 – Real problems over theory
We started by building internal tools: scripts and mini-bots to clean data, follow up on leads, and make sense of finances for service businesses and clinics.
Step 2 – Productizing the best workflows
The most effective workflows became reusable frameworks: MiniCRM Lead-Gen for capture and follow-up, CareClerk for healthcare communication, and SummitAI CFO for owner visibility.
Step 3 – Custom bots & training
Today, we combine those proven templates with custom logic for your stack — plus training so your team owns the system, not the other way around.
