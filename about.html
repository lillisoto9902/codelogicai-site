About CodeLogicAI

CodeLogicAI's AI studio is built by operators, not theorists. We focus on practical automations that make everyday work lighter for owners, admins, and care teams.

Instead of chasing hype, we build and maintain real systems: CRM bots that catch every lead, CareClerk for hospitals and clinics, CFO-style insight bots for financial clarity, and AI Certification Bootcamps that help teams use AI safely and confidently.

What we actually do

  • CodeLogicAI CRM Bot – lead capture, qualification, follow-up, and CRM updates.
  • CareClerk for hospitals & clinics – intake, scheduling, reminders, and safer patient communication.
  • CFO insights bot – simple, human-readable summaries of revenue, pipeline, and activity.
  • AI Certification Bootcamp – training for teams on how to partner with AI instead of fighting it.
  • Managed hosting & servers – we keep your bots online, updated, and monitored.

How we work

Every project starts with finding the weakness: missed leads, staff burnout, messy data, or lack of visibility. From there, we design one clear workflow at a time, then plug in AI where it actually helps.

  • We map your current tools and processes.
  • We design flows that respect your policies and guardrails.
  • We build, test, and iterate alongside your team.
  • We document everything and stay on as your support layer.

For both business & healthcare

Our background sits at the intersection of small business ops and healthcare workflows. That’s why we care about things like compliance, handoffs, and real human impact — not just how “smart” the model looks in a demo.

Whether you’re running a growing service business or a hospital department, our goal is the same: make your systems feel calmer, clearer, and more under control.
