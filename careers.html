Careers at CodeLogicAI

CodeLogicAI is a small, focused studio building AI systems that actually help people: bots that catch leads, support patients, and give owners real visibility into their work.

We’re not hiring at scale, but we are always paying attention to people who understand both technology and real-world operations — especially in small business and healthcare.

Who we love working with

You don’t have to check every box, but if some of these sound like you, you’re probably our kind of person:

  • You care about outcomes, not just shiny tools or hype.
  • You’re comfortable talking to owners, admins, or care teams in plain language.
  • You enjoy mapping messy processes and turning them into clear flows.
  • You’re curious about AI and automation and willing to learn quickly.
  • You document what you do so others can rely on it after you.

Example roles as we grow

We’re still early, but these are the types of roles we expect to add over time:

  • Bot implementation specialist – set up and configure bots for clients.
  • Client success & onboarding – help businesses and clinics adopt their new workflows.
  • Technical writer / trainer – create guides, SOPs, and training for teams.
  • Backend or automation engineer – connect bots to CRMs, EHRs, and internal systems.
  • Healthcare workflow specialist – help design safe, realistic CareClerk flows.

Most roles are remote-friendly with strong written communication and async collaboration.

How to introduce yourself

If you’d like to be considered for future opportunities, you can send:

  • Your resume or LinkedIn profile.
  • A short note about the kind of work you want to do.
  • Any examples of projects, automations, documentation, or systems you’ve helped build.

Email everything to: codelogicai.llc@gmail.com with the subject line “Careers at CodeLogicAI”.

If there’s a strong fit, we’ll keep your information on file and reach out as roles open up.
